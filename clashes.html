Required-course clashes

Each program's suggested 4-year plan, for every entry year from Fall 2020 to Fall 2026, checked against this year's class schedule (Fall semesters against Fall 2026-27, Spring against Spring 2026-27). A semester clashes when no choice of sections lets a student take all of its required courses without two of them meeting at the same time.

Fix list

Course pairs whose sections always overlap, wherever a suggested plan puts them in the same semester. Fix one by moving a section or opening a new one at another hour.

By program and entry year

Semesters with a clash, out of 8. Click a square to see that plan.

Plan

The plan is the one SU Planner suggests (required courses in prerequisite order, by course level, in a season they run in), but kept in curriculum order: SU Planner itself pushes a clashing course to a later semester, which hides the clash from students and delays their plan. Elective placeholders, PROJ courses (times arranged after registration) and summer internships are not checked.
